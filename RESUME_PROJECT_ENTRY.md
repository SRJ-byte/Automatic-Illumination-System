# Resume Entry --- LDR-Based Automatic Illumination System

**LDR-Based Automatic Illumination System** - Designed and developed a
light-sensitive automatic illumination circuit using an LDR, transistor,
LED, resistor(s), and 9V supply. - Prototyped and validated the circuit
on a breadboard before implementing the design as a custom PCB. -
Designed the schematic and PCB layout using EasyEDA and carried out
hardware assembly and testing. - Gained hands-on experience in
sensor-based automation, transistor switching, PCB design, and hardware
troubleshooting.
